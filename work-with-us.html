Who we work with

Bring a similar session to your…

🏢 Workplace

Corporate wellness that goes beyond a talk. Help teams notice fatigue and stress and make better decisions about rest, movement and recovery.

🎓 Campus / institution

Interactive sessions for students and staff on self-regulation, awareness and healthy active habits.

🏃 Running community

Runner Decision Tests, club sessions and crew training so members run smart, not just hard.

🎪 Sports event / EO

An engaging safety and wellness booth or stage session, like the Run Safe Playground, for your race or event.

How it works

From a first chat to a program that fits

  1. Tell us about your group: who they are, what they do, and what you'd like to change.
  2. We have a short conversation to understand your goals and constraints.
  3. We design a program around real situations your people face, using Feel → Think → Decide.
  4. We deliver it as interactive experiences, not lectures, and people leave with something to keep using.
