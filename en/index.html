What we do

A healthy, active life only counts if you can keep it up.

Lots of people start exercising. Fewer keep going. We help with both: getting started, and making it sustainable.

Feel → Think → Decide

Your body always sends signals. We help you read them.

Fatigue, pain, poor sleep, thirst. The signals are almost always there. What's usually missing is a pause to notice them before you decide.

  • Feel: notice the signalPay attention to what your body is actually telling you instead of brushing it off.
  • Think: understand the causeIs this just effort, or is something wrong? Is it your body, your goal or your ego deciding?
  • Decide: make the smarter callKeep going, slow down, stop and reassess, or rest. For today and for tomorrow.

Why we care about running safely

We move to get healthier. Let's not let it turn into the opposite.

Running is one of the easiest ways to start an active life. But:

Injury

Run but get injured?

Then it won't be sustainable. An injury can stop someone's active lifestyle for months, or for good.

Fainting

Run but faint?

That's dangerous, for you and for the people around you. Most of the time the body gave signals first.

Collapse

Worse, someone collapses?

Then every second counts, and someone nearby needs to know what to do.

That's why we're at UGM Trail Run 2026 with our partner ADEM: raising runners' awareness so their active lifestyle stays healthy and sustainable. It's one of our programs, not the only one.
