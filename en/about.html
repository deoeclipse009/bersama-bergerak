We help people start an active lifestyle, and keep it going.

Bersama Bergerak (“moving together”) is a Movement & Active Living Initiative. We support people to start a healthy, active lifestyle and to make it sustainable. Our work is for students, professionals, communities and organisations, not only runners.

The point of moving is to get healthier. Not the opposite.

Starting is hard. Keeping going is harder. People get injured, burn out, or push past their body's signals.

So we don't just tell people to move more. We help them build the awareness and habits that make an active life safe and sustainable, through short, practical, hands-on programs built around real situations.

Our awareness program for runners at UGM Trail Run 2026 is one example of that work.

At a glance

  • Mission: help people start a healthy, active lifestyle and keep it sustainable
  • Focus: self-regulation, awareness and everyday habits
  • Approach: Feel → Think → Decide
  • Format: interactive experiences, workshops and programs, not lectures
  • For: students, campuses, workplaces, communities, institutions and sports events, not only runners
  • Find us: @bersamabergerak.id

Feel → Think → Decide

A simple framework anyone can use before, during and after a run, or on any busy day.

FEEL

Notice what your body is telling you: fatigue, pain, poor sleep, thirst. Don't dismiss the signal.

→
THINK

Ask what might be causing it, and whether this is something to push through or something that needs attention.

→
DECIDE

Make the smarter call for today: keep going, slow down, stop and reassess, or rest.

It's not about finding the “one right answer”. It's about making a better decision when your body, your goals and the people around you are pulling in different directions.

Our principles

01

Behavior beats information

Knowing isn't the same as doing. We design for the moment of decision, not the classroom.

02

Experiences, not homework

People shouldn't have to write long reflections or sit through a seminar.

03

Not medical advice

We don't diagnose. We help people recognise signals and know when to seek help, and we work with medical partners for that side.

04

Sustainable over extreme

Run smart, not just hard. The goal is to keep moving for years.

“Bersama” means together. Safer habits spread through communities, crews and teams, not only individuals.

05

Something to take home

Every experience ends with something practical you can keep using, like our free Runner Reset Guide.
