Run Safe Playground

Not a health-education booth. A playground for runners: come to test yourself, play, learn something, and go home with something useful. Protect Yourself. Protect Others.

When & where Try it online

Five steps, a few minutes

PLAY→ TEST→ LEARN→ TAKE HOME→ CONNECT

Runners come to race pack collection to pick up their pack, meet friends, take photos and have fun, not to sit and read long cards. So everything here takes 1 to 3 minutes.

Three zones to explore

Front of the booth: “Can you pass the Runner Safety Test?”

Zone 1 · Come & play · 1 min

🎡 Spin the Wheel: Run Safe Quiz

Spin, get a question, answer out loud. Fun first, curiosity second, conversation third.

  • “Pain or push?”
  • “Rest day or lazy day?”
  • “Cramp: keep running or stop?”
  • “Can you recognise an emergency?”
  • “What should you do first?”
Zone 2 · Think · 2–3 min

🧠 The Runner Decision Test

Bersama Bergerak's hero experience. You're given a real running situation and choose what you'd do. Then a facilitator walks you through Feel → Think → Decide.

We don't say “this is the right answer”. We ask: how do you make a better decision when your body and your ego are telling you different things?

Try a scenario
Zone 3 · Act · 2–3 min

❤️ CPR Battle

ADEM's hero experience: “Can you save a life?” Practise CPR on a manikin for 30 to 60 seconds, get scored and battle a friend.

Open to runners, pacers, sweepers, marshals, communities and anyone visiting. Learn more at ademsigap.com.

KM 8. What would you do?

Your pace is still good. You have to work tomorrow. Your friend shouts: “Gas! Only 2 km left!” And your knee is starting to feel uncomfortable.

  • A. Gas. Don't be weak.
  • B. Slow down & assess.
  • C. Stop & reassess.
  • D. Depends.
There's no trick answer.

What matters is how you decide. That's what we practise together:

FEEL

What is my knee actually telling me?

THINK

What's driving me: my body, my goal, or my ego?

DECIDE

What's the smarter call, today and for tomorrow?
