UGM Trail Run 2026 · 25–27 September

Where to find us

Three days, two locations. Visit us at race pack collection at GIK and at the Race Village in Hargobinangun.

Times marked tentative are still being confirmed with the UGM Trail Run organising team. Please follow the official event announcements for the final rundown.

Run Safe Playground · Booth #9
