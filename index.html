Run smart, not just hard.

Bersama Bergerak helps people listen to their bodies and make better everyday decisions about how they move, push, rest and recover. We turn that into fun, hands-on programs for runners, communities, campuses and workplaces.

Why Bersama Bergerak

Your running problem may not be your training plan.

It may be your decision-making. Most injuries, burnouts and bad race days start with a signal we chose to ignore. That's the part we work on.

Self-regulation, not just information

We don't hand out leaflets. We help people build the habit of checking in with their body before they decide.

Short, fun and practical

1 to 3 minute experiences you can take part in between picking up your race pack and meeting your friends. No seminar feel.

Made to go further

The same approach works for running communities, campuses and workplaces. We design programs that fit each group.
