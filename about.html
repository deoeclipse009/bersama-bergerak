About Bersama Bergerak

We design programs that help people make better everyday decisions.

Bersama Bergerak (“moving together”) is a Movement & Active Living Initiative. We focus on behavior: how people notice what their body is telling them, and what they decide to do next.

What we do in one sentence

We don't just give people information. We help them make better decisions.

There's plenty of health information out there. What's harder is using it at the right moment: at KM 8 when your knee starts to hurt, when you've slept four hours before a long run, or when your friends are shouting “Gas!”.

That moment is where Bersama Bergerak works. Our programs are short, practical and built around real situations, so people can use them straight away.

At a glance

  • Focus: self-regulation, awareness and everyday habits
  • Approach: Feel → Think → Decide
  • Format: interactive experiences, workshops and programs, not lectures
  • For: running communities, workplaces, campuses, institutions and sports events
  • Find us: @bersamabergerak.id
Our approach

Feel → Think → Decide

A simple framework anyone can use before, during and after a run, or on any busy day.

FEEL

Notice what your body is telling you: fatigue, pain, poor sleep, thirst. Don't dismiss the signal.

→
THINK

Ask what might be causing it, and whether this is something to push through or something to respect.

→
DECIDE

Make the smarter call for today: keep going, slow down, stop and reassess, or rest.

It's not about finding the “one right answer”. It's about making a better decision when your body, your goals and the people around you are pulling in different directions.

What we believe

Our principles

01

Behavior beats information

Knowing isn't the same as doing. We design for the moment of decision, not the classroom.

02

Experiences, not homework

People shouldn't have to write long reflections or sit through a seminar. If it takes more than a few minutes, it has to earn it.

03

Not medical advice

We don't diagnose. We help people recognise signals and know when to seek help, and we work with medical partners for that side.

04

Sustainable over extreme

Run smart, not just hard. The goal is to keep moving for years, not to win one day and get injured the next.

05

Moving together

“Bersama” means together. Safer habits spread through communities, crews and teams, not only individuals.

06

Something to take home

Every experience ends with something practical you can keep using, like our free Runner Reset Guide.
